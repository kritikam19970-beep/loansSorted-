Frequently asked questions

Your questions, answered.

Still have questions? We're here to help.

Ask us a question
How much can I borrow?
Borrowing capacity depends on your income, expenses, existing debts, deposit and lender policy. We can help you understand your options.
What documents do I need?
Requirements vary by lender and loan type. We'll give you a clear checklist once we understand your situation, so you only gather what's needed.
Do you charge a fee?
We'll explain how we're paid before you commit to anything, so there are no surprises. Ask us during your free consultation.
Can you help with low deposits?
Often there are options, depending on your circumstances and lender policy. We'll walk you through what may be available, including any grants or schemes you could be eligible for.
How long does the process take?
Timeframes depend on the lender, the complexity of your application and how quickly documents come together. We'll give you a realistic timeline up front and keep you updated along the way.
Can you help self-employed borrowers?
Yes. Lenders assess self-employed income differently, so we'll help you present your situation clearly and explore the options that suit it.
